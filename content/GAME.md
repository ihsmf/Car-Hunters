# CUE HUNTERS — full game description (for an AI content assistant)

> Read this whole file before suggesting any video idea. The game's name is always **CUE HUNTERS**.

## 0. The situation right now (most important)

- The game is **new and unknown**: few players, a TikTok/YouTube account with almost no followers.
- Ideas about the game's internal mechanics have **already failed**: "guess the luck", "look at my rare ball", "rate
  my balls", generic noob vs pro, plain gameplay with a caption. A stranger does not care about luck numbers in a
  game they have never heard of.
- So a good idea must **work for someone who has never heard of the game**. Real people, emotions, a story, real
  stakes, a challenge or a "wow" that needs no explanation. The game is the stage, not the topic.
- The goal of every video: **a stranger watches to the end, then goes to play the game.**

## 1. One-line pitch

A Roblox pool game where you hit the cue ball down a **6-kilometre pool table through 16 different worlds**. The
further it rolls, the rarer the ball you win — up to **1 in 10,000,000** — and the last table ends in a cinematic
**"Birth of a Star"**.

- Roblox link: https://www.roblox.com/games/103508491994866 (title on Roblox: `[ NEW! ] CUE HUNTERS`)
- Genre: simulator / RNG collecting / idle tycoon. Platforms: PC and mobile (most players are on phones).
- Audience: Roblox players aged 9–17, worldwide (English first), plus a RU/CIS audience.

## 2. Core loop

1. **Shoot.** The player steps into the shot zone at the start of the long table and presses **SHOOT** (a timing
   power bar). The cue strikes the cue ball, it flies down the table.
2. **Roll.** The ball rolls along **one continuous table ~6,000 studs long**, made of **75 tables** of 80 studs each.
   The camera follows it. A big **luck counter** counts up live on screen as the ball passes tables.
3. **Drop.** The ball rolls into the pocket of the table where it stops (sparks, shockwave, slow camera on the
   pocket), the screen fades to black.
4. **Reveal.** A short **themed scene** plays (see §5), then the **close-up**: the won ball spins in front of the
   player on a dark background with rays, its name, rarity, income per second and its odds **"1 IN N"** counting up.
5. **Base.** The ball appears on the player's own pool table at their base and **earns cash every second**.
6. **Upgrade.** Cash buys better cues (more luck), dumbbell levels (more strength → longer shots) and table capacity
   (more balls earning at once). Repeat.

## 3. Progression systems

- **Strength = distance.** Strength comes only from **training at the dumbbell** on your base. The character curls the
  dumbbell, every rep (1.2 s) adds strength: a floor shockwave, a glowing arm aura that grows, steam puffs, a rep combo
  counter "x12", and every 10th rep a **"POWER UP!"** burst with a camera kick. The dumbbell has **50 levels** (each
  rep gives 3 × 2^(level−1) strength; level cost 100 × 3^(level−1)).
- **Luck = rarity.** Every table has its own luck range that grows along the hall (table 1 ≈ ×1–1.5, the far tables
  far more; inside a table luck grows linearly with distance). When the ball drops, the **cue multiplies the luck**.
- **Cues (15 tiers, luck multiplier — price):**
  - Wooden ×1 — free; Maple ×1.2 — 400; Ash ×1.4 — 8K; Carbon ×1.65 — 150K; Ebony ×1.9 — 3M;
  - Golden ×2.2 — 60M; Ivory ×2.55 — 1.2B; Crystal ×2.95 — 25B; Inferno ×3.4 — 500B;
  - Frost ×3.9 — 10T; Storm ×4.5 — 200T; Shadow ×5.2 — 4Qa; Celestial ×6 — 80Qa;
  - Cosmic ×7 — 2Qi; **Legend ×100** — 50Qi.

  Bought at the **Cue Shop** booth on the base (a horizontal card carousel).
- **Table capacity:** the base table starts with **8 spots** and grows to **20** (8→10→12→14→16→18→20). The table
  physically gets longer with every level. Prices $1M, $100M, $10B, $1T, $100T, $10Qa. Sign with an UPGRADE button next
  to the table.
- **Inventory:** up to 150 balls; Place / Remove / Sell, "Equip best", sort by income.
- **Rewards:** daily login streak (100 → 15,000 cash over 7 days), playtime gifts at 5 / 15 / 30 / 60 minutes.

## 6. The finale — "Birth of a Star" (the best shot of the game)

Only when the ball reaches the **last table** (~13 s, cinematic, on an open starry sky with nebulae):
1. Everything **freezes**, the camera is low behind the table.
2. **75 balls — one from every table — rise** from the felt.
3. They **swirl into a tilted galaxy** above the table; the camera orbits wide.
4. The galaxy **collapses**, the core of a new star heats up, the camera pushes in.
5. **Supernova:** white flash, 500 sparks, an expanding shockwave ring, camera shake.
6. A **legendary ball is born from the light** and flies into the camera → the close-up card.
7. The player gets a permanent **super aura** (rainbow ring at the feet, light) visible to everyone.
