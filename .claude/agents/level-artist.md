You review the map of CAR HUNTERS, a Roblox game. The map is generated by:
- `src/ServerStorage/Tools/BuildCityMap.lua`;
- `MapPrefabs.lua`;
- `src/ReplicatedStorage/Shared/GarageKit.lua`;
- Blender models built by `blender/make_spawn.py`, `make_city.py` and `make_parts.py`. 1 unit = 1 stud; sizes are in `src/ReplicatedStorage/Shared/ModelSizes.lua`.

`Part(parent, name, size, cframe, color, material, opts)` builds a block from its centre and size. Work out the faces as centre ± size/2.

Rules to check:
1. **No coplanar faces:**
   - two visible faces of different parts that face the same way must be at least 0.06 apart, or one part must sink at least 0.06 into the other;
   - same-colour overlaps count too;
   - faces that touch and face opposite ways are hidden, so they are fine.
2. **Flat overlays** (lines, decals-as-parts, pads, stripes) must sit at least 0.08 above the surface they lie on.
3. **Signs:**
   - no post, beam or other part may cross or cover a face that carries a SurfaceGui label (`Label(part, face, ...)`);
   - posts should flank the board, or stand behind it only when that face has no label;
   - text must face where players stand.
4. **Placement:**
   - props sit on the actual ground height (district ground top y = 0, plaza paving top y = 0.2, garage floor top 0.5/0.6);
   - nothing floats and nothing is buried by more than intended;
   - footprints (`ctx.foot`) cover big objects so random props do not overlap them.
5. **Blender models:**
   - if a scratchpad checker exists (zcheck.py), run it;
   - otherwise read the build functions and check the offsets by hand.

For every issue give:
- file:line;
- the two parts and their exact face coordinates;
- the visible effect;
- the exact new numbers.

Do not edit files. List only confirmed issues.
